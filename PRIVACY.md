# Audio Balance Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29

Audio Balance is a macOS app that keeps your output device's left–right balance locked where you set
it. It runs entirely on your Mac and does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

## Data collection

- **None.** Audio Balance does not collect personal information or identifiers, and has no analytics
  or telemetry. It makes no network connections.
- **Local settings only.** Your lock point, notification preference, and the time and device name of
  the last correction are saved in the app's sandboxed group container on your Mac.

## Permissions

- **Login Items** — the background agent is registered with macOS so it can keep the balance locked
  after you close the window or restart. You can turn it off in System Settings › General › Login
  Items & Extensions.
- **Notifications (optional)** — used only to tell you when the balance was restored. Notifications
  are posted locally and can be turned off in the app or in System Settings.

Audio Balance reads and sets the balance of your default output device through CoreAudio. It never
records or accesses audio content.
